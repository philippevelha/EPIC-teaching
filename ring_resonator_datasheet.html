Ring Resonator (RING_RES_STD)

Silicon Photonic Add-Drop Filter
PDK Version 2.1 | Process: SiP220 | Document Rev. 1.3

1. Component Overview

The RING_RES_STD is a standard single-mode silicon photonic ring resonator designed for wavelength-selective filtering, modulation, and sensing applications. The device features evanescent coupling between a bus waveguide and a circular ring cavity, providing sharp resonance characteristics in the C-band and L-band wavelength ranges.

3. Design Equations

Free Spectral Range (FSR)

FSR = λ² / (2πR·ng)

Quality Factor (Q)

Q = 2πngR / (λ·α·L)

where α is the propagation loss coefficient and L is the round-trip length

Resonance Condition

λres = neff·L / m

where m is the resonance order (integer)
